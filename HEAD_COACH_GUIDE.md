# PlayHub — Head Coach Guide (Simple Walkthrough)

A short, plain-English guide to what you can do as a **Head Coach** and how. No
technical knowledge needed.

---

## What a head coach does

As a head coach you run **your sport at your center**. PlayHub automatically
shows you only the batches, students, and coaches for **your center and your
sport** — you're never shown other sports or other locations.

**You can:**
- See and run your **batches** (classes)
- See and add **students** at your center
- See and add **coaches** for your sport
- **Enrol** students into a class, **mark attendance**, and **record performance**
- **Message parents**
- **Invite** new coaches and trainers

**Your academy admin handles (not you):** fees and payments (you won't see
money), deleting students or coaches, and giving parents/students their own app
logins.

---

## The app at a glance

Along the bottom of the screen you have five tabs:

**Home · Batches · Notices · Messages · Alerts**

- **Home** — your daily dashboard.
- **Batches** — your list of classes.
- **Notices** — send and read announcements.
- **Messages** — chat.
- **Alerts** — your notifications.

---

## Your Home screen

The Home tab gives you:

- A greeting and three quick numbers: **Today** (classes today), **My batches**,
  and **Students**.
- **Today's sessions** — tap it to mark attendance for today's classes.
- A **Manage** area with shortcuts: **Students**, **Coaches**, and
  **Invite staff**.
- An **attendance trend** chart for the last few weeks.

---

## Batches — your classes

1. Tap the **Batches** tab. You'll see every class for your center and sport.
   **The number on each card is how many students are enrolled.**
2. Tap **New batch** to create a class (pick the days, times, and how many
   students it can hold).
3. Tap any batch card to open it.

### Opening a batch (this is where you see who's in the class)

When you open a batch you get everything about it:

- The top shows **Enrolled**, **Capacity**, and **days per week**.
- The **Enrolment** list shows **all the students in the class**, grouped into
  **Active**, **Waitlist**, and **Withdrawn** — each group shows a count.
- **Enrol** a student, **Withdraw** one, or **Transfer** one to another class.
- **Mark attendance** (button at the top).
- Tap a student to open their details, or tap **Message parent** to contact the
  family.

> **Want "how many students are in a batch, and who?"** — the number is on the
> batch card, and the full list is inside the batch under **Enrolment**.

---

## Students

1. On **Home**, in the **Manage** area, tap **Students**.
2. You'll see the students at your center. Use the **search** box or the
   **filters** to find someone.
3. Tap **New student** to add one (name, parent name and phone, etc.).
4. Tap any student to view or edit their details, or to enrol them into one of
   your batches.

> Adding a student just creates their record — it doesn't email anyone. Giving a
> student or parent their own app login is handled by your academy admin.

---

## Coaches

1. On **Home → Manage → Coaches**.
2. You'll see the coaches for your sport and center.
3. Tap **New coach** to add one — then you can put them in charge of a batch.

---

## Parents

There's no separate "parents" list. You reach a parent **through their child**:

- A student's **parent name** shows on the student's row.
- Open a student (or a batch roster) and tap **Message parent** to contact the
  family.

---

## Inviting coaches and trainers

1. On **Home → Manage → Invite staff** (or the person-add icon at the top right).
2. Enter their **email** and choose **Coach** or **Trainer**.
3. They get an email, set their own password, and log in to their own screens.

---

## Marking attendance and recording performance

- **Attendance:** on **Home** tap **Today's sessions**, or open a batch and tap
  **Mark attendance**. Tick each student present/absent and save.
- **Performance:** open a student or a batch and record an assessment or attach a
  photo/video.

---

## Quick reference — "Where do I…?"

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| See my classes | **Batches** tab |
| See how many students are in a class | The number on the batch card (or open it) |
| See who's in a class | Open the batch → **Enrolment** list |
| Create a class | **Batches → New batch** |
| Add a student to a class | Open the batch → **Enrol** |
| See my students | **Home → Manage → Students** |
| See / add coaches | **Home → Manage → Coaches** |
| Message a parent | Open a student or batch roster → **Message parent** |
| Invite a coach / trainer | **Home → Manage → Invite staff** |
| Mark attendance | **Home → Today's sessions**, or **Batch → Mark attendance** |
| Record performance | Open a student or a batch |

---

## A typical day

1. Open **Home** → tap **Today's sessions** → mark who's present.
2. Check the **Batches** tab to see a class roster or enrol a student.
3. Message a parent if you need to (open the student → **Message parent**).

That's the whole head coach flow.
